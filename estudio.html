Tomás Schlossberg

Tomás Schlossberg is an architect based between Buenos Aires and Paris. He studied architecture at the Universidad de Buenos Aires and later moved to Paris, where he continued his training and worked at Laplace.

His work moves between architecture, interior design and art — each project shaped by its site and the people who inhabit it, followed closely from the first sketch to the last detail.
